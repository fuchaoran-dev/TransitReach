# MD8-6 — Group Outing Invitation and Personal Pass

- **Parent:** Epic 8 extension for Epic 6
- **Priority:** Must Have
- **Status:** Ready for complete implementation independently of the unfinished Epic 2 optimiser.

## Purpose

> As a car-free resident planning with other people
>
> I want the agreed meeting to arrive as an invitation and a personal pass
>
> So that I know when I should leave, whether my journey still works, and what the group needs to know without exposing my route.

This document contains the Epic 8 behaviour associated with Epic 6 meeting rooms. Every member receives a personal point-to-point plan from their own starting point to the group's confirmed place and time. It does not require a multi-stop plan.

## Delivery Decision

- MD8-6 is a complete, independently deliverable scope and may be implemented before Epic 2.
- Every acceptance criterion from 8-6.1.1 through 8-6.5.3 is part of the MD8-6 delivery.
- **“Add errands” is not part of MD8-6.** Original Epic 8 criterion 8.5.3 is owned by `MD8-2.md` and remains blocked until the Epic 2 Trip Planner is complete.
- MD8-6 must not show an enabled or placeholder **“Add errands”** action. MD8-2 will add that integration later.
- The shared check and pass model should be reusable by MD8-2, but MD8-6 must not wait for MD8-2 to define or implement it.

## Dependencies

- Epic 6 supplies the room, membership, participant identity, starting point and ranked meeting places.
- Epic 3 point-to-point routing supplies journey legs, map geometry and walking directions.
- Epic 4 supplies typical bus-delay information and explicit unsupported results.
- Epic 7 supplies forecast periods and walking-weather context.
- Essential Services supplies reliable opening hours where available.
- MD8-6 implements the shared Epic 8 pass renderer, storage and plan-check model; MD8-2 can reuse those foundations later.

Epic 6 must be extended to persist a confirmed meeting place, an agreed arrival time and a plan version. A place that is only selected in one browser is not a confirmed group plan.

## US 8-6.1 — Confirm the Group Plan

> As a meeting-room member
>
> I want the group to confirm one place and time
>
> So that every personal pass is based on the same agreement.

### 8-6.1.1 Confirmed Place and Time

Given the room has selected a ranked meeting place, When a place and meeting time are confirmed, Then the room stores the place identity, display name, coordinate, agreed arrival time and a new plan version.

### 8-6.1.2 Shared Confirmation

Given a meeting is confirmed, When any member views the room, Then the same confirmed place and time are shown to every member.

### 8-6.1.3 Changed Agreement Invalidates Passes

Given personal passes already exist, When the confirmed place or time changes, Then the room receives a new plan version and every earlier pass is marked outdated until recalculated.

### 8-6.1.4 Unconfirmed Rooms Stay in Planning

Given a room has no confirmed place or time, When a member opens its link, Then the room remains in planning mode and does not claim that an invitation or pass is ready.

---

## US 8-6.2 — Check Each Member's Journey

> As a group member
>
> I want my journey checked against the agreed arrival time
>
> So that I know when to leave and where my journey may fail.

### 8-6.2.1 Personal Arrive-by Journey

Given I have set my starting point and the meeting is confirmed, When my check runs, Then a journey is calculated from my own starting point to the meeting place, arriving by the agreed time.

### 8-6.2.2 Spare Time at Each Connection

Given my journey has more than one leg, When the check runs, Then every connection shows the estimated spare time between arriving and the next departure.

### 8-6.2.3 Typical Bus Delay Applied

Given a bus leg has delay history, When the check runs, Then the typical delay for its route, stop and hour is carried into later connections and my estimated arrival.

### 8-6.2.4 Arrival and Closing Margins

Given my journey is checked, Then the pass shows the estimated margin before the agreed meeting time. If the meeting place has reliable opening hours, it also shows the margin before closing; otherwise it shows **“Hours unknown”**.

### 8-6.2.5 Weather Exposure

Given a forecast is available, When the pass opens, Then it shows the relevant forecast period, its source and my total walking time within that period.

### 8-6.2.6 Weak Point Named

Given the check has run, When the result is shown, Then my tightest connection, arrival margin or closing-time margin is named in one sentence with the figures behind it. No single overall score is shown.

### 8-6.2.7 No Data Is Not “Fine”

Given a leg has no applicable delay history, When the result is shown, Then that leg is marked **“Not checked”** and looks different from a checked leg that passed.

## US 8-6.3 — Invitation and Personal Pass

> As a group member
>
> I want the invitation to open into my own pass
>
> So that the shared agreement becomes a journey I can follow.

### 8-6.3.1 Group Invitation

Given the room has a confirmed place and time, When a member opens the room link, Then an invitation naming that place and time is shown and can be opened into a pass.

### 8-6.3.2 Personal Pass for Each Member

Given a member opens the invitation, When their pass is produced, Then it uses only that member's starting point and shows when they should leave to arrive by the agreed time.

### 8-6.3.3 Pass Contents

Given the personal pass is open, Then it shows:

- an estimated leave time;
- the agreed meeting place and time;
- every journey leg in order;
- the estimated arrival and arrival margin;
- walking directions and total walking time;
- applicable weather context; and
- the named weak point.

### 8-6.3.4 Times Are Estimates

Given any journey time is displayed, Then it uses estimated wording, such as **“Leave around 5:40 pm”**, and is never described as a guaranteed scheduled departure.

### 8-6.3.5 Canonical Pass Format

Given a group meeting produces a pass, Then it uses the shared Epic 8 pass structure. Group-specific invitation content wraps the pass but does not create a separate pass design.

### 8-6.3.6 Privacy in the Group

Given several members hold passes, When the group pass view is shown, Then it shows only each person's display name and coarse arrival status, such as **“Ready”**, **“Check needed”** or **“Not checked”**.

No member's route, journey legs, starting-point label or starting-point coordinate is shown to another member. This restriction must be enforced by the data-access boundary, not only hidden with CSS.

## US 8-6.4 — Keep It and Find It Again

> As a group member
>
> I want to save my pass and return to it on the day
>
> So that I do not have to reconstruct the meeting journey.

### 8-6.4.1 Download

Given my pass is open, When I select **“Download”**, Then an image is saved containing my leave time, journey legs, meeting place and weak point, readable without an internet connection.

The downloaded image must not include another member's private information.

### 8-6.4.2 Group QR Code

Given a group pass is open or downloaded, When its QR code is scanned, Then it opens the room identified by an opaque room reference.

On a device that can restore the same member identity, the member's live pass opens. On another device, the invitation opens and that person must join or provide their own starting point before a personal pass can be calculated.

### 8-6.4.3 My Passes

Given the member has produced one or more passes, When they open **“My passes”** on the same device, Then each upcoming group pass is listed and can be reopened.

### 8-6.4.4 Re-check on Reopening

Given a pass is reopened before the meeting, When newer forecast or delay information is available, Then the personal check runs again and states whether the plan still holds.

### 8-6.4.5 Nothing Private in the Code

Given a group QR code is generated, Then it contains no member identifier, starting point, route, journey geometry or personal check result. It contains only the room reference needed to open the invitation flow.

---

## US 8-6.5 — Fix It and Carry On

> As a group member
>
> I want to act on my weak point from the pass
>
> So that I can improve my journey without exposing it to the group.

### 8-6.5.1 Suggested Personal Fix

Given a personal weak point is named, When a supported fix exists, Then the pass may offer **“Leave earlier”** or **“Choose another journey”** without changing the agreed group place or time.

### 8-6.5.2 Suggest Another Meeting Time

Given my journey cannot reliably reach the meeting, When I select **“Suggest another time”**, Then I return to the room with a proposed time. The shared meeting time changes only after it is explicitly confirmed as a new group agreement.

### 8-6.5.3 Re-check After a Change

Given my journey selection changes or the room confirms a new place or time, When a replacement journey is calculated, Then the check runs again and my pass updates to the new plan version.

## Required Implementation Order

MD8-6 must be implemented from its data and privacy foundations outward. Pass presentation must not be built on temporary local-only confirmation or on data access that exposes another member's origin.

1. **Database confirmation model**
   - Persist the confirmed meeting place, agreed arrival time and plan version on the room.
   - Add controlled confirmation/update operations and realtime propagation.
   - Make any place or time change invalidate passes created for the previous plan version.
2. **Privacy boundary**
   - Separate the current member's private origin and journey data from the room's shared status projection.
   - Ensure another member's origin, route and personal check result are not returned by the group-pass data boundary.
   - Move any calculation that requires all participant coordinates behind an appropriately controlled RPC or backend boundary.
3. **Arrive-by routing and plan check**
   - Add arrive-by point-to-point routing for the agreed meeting time.
   - Implement delay propagation, connection spare time, arrival and closing margins, weather exposure, unsupported-data states and weak-point selection as testable domain logic.
4. **Invitation and personal pass UI**
   - Build the invitation only after a confirmed room plan exists.
   - Render the current member's pass from persisted confirmation and checked journey data.
5. **Keep and reopen**
   - Add My Passes, download, room-only QR data and re-check-on-open behaviour.
6. **Fix actions**
   - Add leave-earlier, alternative-journey and suggest-another-time actions, followed by a complete re-check.

Database confirmation and the privacy boundary are release prerequisites. They must be complete before the pass UI is treated as an implementation of MD8-6.

## Implementation Constraints Identified in the Current Project

- `meeting_rooms` currently stores a time budget but no confirmed venue or agreed meeting time.
- The selected meeting place currently exists only in `MeetingPointPage` state and is not a room-level agreement.
- The point-to-point routing client currently sends `arriveBy=false`; personal group passes require an arrive-by query or an equivalent verified calculation.
- Current meeting-room reads expose participant coordinates to other members and the UI draws their markers and areas. The pass privacy requirement needs a safe member-status projection and a data-access design that does not return other members' private route or origin data.

## Out of Scope

- Completing the Epic 2 multi-stop optimiser.
- Adding errands, reordering errands or calculating private multi-stop additions; these belong to MD8-2.
- Showing another member's route or starting point in the invitation, group pass or downloaded image.
- Treating weather forecasts, historical delays or modelled journey times as guarantees.
